Our Story

Made slowly,
on purpose.

Aurèle was born in a small studio in Skopje, from a desire to make jewellery that doesn't follow trends — only instinct. Every piece passes through our hands three times before it reaches yours.

We believe in less. Fewer pieces, made better. 16 designs, not 160. Each one chosen deliberately, each one a quiet statement.

"We make jewellery for the woman who already knows who she is."
